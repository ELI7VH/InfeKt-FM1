Install InfeKt-FM1 1.19

The installer writes the firmware and, if you like, the factory banks (into banks A and B) and Sete's nine wavetables. InfeKt-FM1 runs on both FM-1 hardware versions, including the newer one (another flash chip, UC25HQ80; GLO › ABOUT shows FLASH B36014). On those newer units the optional m-bootloader is not installed: the installer refuses it.

What you need

  • Chrome or Edge on a computer. Other browsers cannot talk to the FM-1.
  • A USB data cable. Some cables only charge.
  • Your FM-1, switched on and plugged in directly.

How it goes

  1. Already using InfeKt-FM1? Back up your banks first: Patches tab, Back up all banks.
  2. Click Connect in the installer below and allow MIDI.
  3. Keep or untick the two options: Install factory banks (they replace banks A and B) and Install Sete's wavetables.
  4. Click Install. The page writes the firmware, then the wavetables and the banks. Do not pull the cable.
  5. The FM-1 restarts as InfeKt-FM1.

Good to know

  • Something went wrong? Do not panic. Start the installer again. The way back to the original firmware is on its first tab.
  • Factory banks and wavetables can also be installed later, from the Patches and Wavetables tabs.
  • The installer can also add an open bootloader with a rescue menu. Read its note before you tick it.
