Support

Something not working as expected? Check the known issues below first. If yours is not there, look through the issues on GitHub or open a new one.

Known issues

Some patches crackle or distort

What you hear: a crackling or harsh distortion on chords, mostly with pads and with sound banks that are programmed very loud.

Why: the patch is too loud inside the synth. Chords then clip before the volume knob, so turning MASTER down does not help.

What helps: lower the part volume. Hold OCT− (that is SHIFT) and turn ALGO down, to about 50–60. This works for every patch. Then turn MASTER up instead. You can also lower the patch's own volume and save the patch.

In 1.19.5 the part volume goes back to its standard value when you switch off.

The factory sound banks are missing

What you see: after installing, banks A and B are empty and only the INIT sound plays.

Why: the factory banks and wavetables are not inside the firmware file. The firmware fills its space on the FM-1 completely, so they live in the sound memory like your own patches. They are included in the Install page and in the installer package (zip).

What helps: install from the Install page with the two boxes ticked, and wait until the page says it is done: the banks are copied after the FM-1 has restarted. Already installed? Open the installer's Patches tab and use the button that installs the factory banks. No reinstall is needed.

Report a problem

Open an issue on GitHub: GitHub issues

It helps to say: the firmware version (GLO › ABOUT), what you did, what happened, and which patch if a sound is involved. After a crash, the installer's first tab can read a crash report from the FM-1.
