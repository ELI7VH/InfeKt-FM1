Try it in the browser

This is the InfeKt-FM1 firmware 1.19.8 itself, running in your browser on a drawn FM-1: the same menus, sequencer, drums and sound, with the factory banks and Sete's wavetables. Click Switch on, then play the keys, turn the knobs (drag up and down, or the mouse wheel) and press the buttons as on the device. A MIDI keyboard works in Chrome and Edge.

Changes are lost when you reload. Not here: Bluetooth, USB audio and saving to a device. Best on a computer; on a phone hold it sideways. Open it in its own tab.
